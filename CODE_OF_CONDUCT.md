# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The HackJudge ecosystem is built to support fair, technically rigorous, and collaborative hackathon experiences. We are committed to providing a welcoming environment for everyone who contributes, reports issues, reviews code, or participates in discussions.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and assume good faith.
- Give actionable, evidence-based technical feedback.
- Welcome different experience levels and perspectives.
- Protect participant data, credentials, and private infrastructure details.
- Disclose security concerns privately and responsibly.
- Keep discussions focused on improving reliability, fairness, accessibility, and maintainability.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Manipulating rankings, submissions, or evaluation data.
- Attempting unauthorized access to Supabase, judging services, or deployment infrastructure.
- Introducing malicious code, secrets, tracking, or deceptive behavior.
- Deliberately disrupting live leaderboard synchronization or hackathon operations.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, demonstrations, and other spaces where someone represents the HackJudge ecosystem.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer through a trusted private communication channel. Do not create a public issue containing sensitive personal or security information.

Include relevant context, links, dates, and supporting evidence when safe to do so.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may clarify, remove, or close contributions; restrict participation; or take other reasonable action in response to violations. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

## Maintainer responsibility

Maintainers should apply this policy consistently, prioritize participant safety and system integrity, and avoid retaliation against good-faith reporters.
